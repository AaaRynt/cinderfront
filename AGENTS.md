## HARD HUMAN-VISIBILITY CONSTRAINT

Never create, model, render, animate, imply through silhouettes, or procedurally generate visible human characters.

This includes:

- infantry;
- pilots;
- sailors;
- deck crews;
- ground crews;
- civilians;
- rescue personnel;
- casualties;
- humanoid silhouettes;
- people inside visible cockpits;
- ejection sequences;
- parachuting personnel.

In particular, never generate any human character requiring articulated limb animation.

All activity must be represented through vehicles, machinery, doors, ramps, lights, weapons, radar systems, ships, aircraft, environmental effects, and infrastructure.

Cockpits may use dark or reflective glazing and must not require visible occupants.
